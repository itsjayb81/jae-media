Questions

Before you buy

How do I get the documents?

After payment you receive a download link by email, usually within a few minutes and always within one business day. Check your spam folder if it has not arrived.

Can I edit them?

Yes. They are standard Word documents. Replace the bracketed details and change anything that does not match how you work. Auditors check that your documents match your practice.

Do these guarantee I pass my audit?

No template can promise that. The audit looks at how you actually work, not just your paperwork. These kits give you a complete, well organised starting point.

Is this legal advice?

No. These are templates and general information. Check current requirements with the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, and get professional advice for your situation.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Reply to your purchase email and pay the difference between kits.
