Paint decontamination and clay bar

What a wash leaves behind. Do the bag test — if it feels gritty, it needs this.

  • Iron fallout remover for embedded brake dust and rail dust
  • Tar and tree sap dissolved off, not scrubbed
  • Clay bar over every panel until the paint is glass-smooth
  • Has to happen before any wax, sealant or coating will bond properly
  • Darker cars can show some marring or swirling afterwards and may need correction — every car and colour is different
From $180Usually done as part of a full detail or before a coating. On its own for paint that feels gritty but does not need correcting.
