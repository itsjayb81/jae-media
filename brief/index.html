Before you start

How it works, so there are no surprises. Please read it.

  1. You buy your own web address. In your name, with your ABN, on your card. We never buy it for you, and here is why:
    • It is yours. Nobody else can hold it, sell it or take it away.
    • You stay in control. You can move it anywhere, any time.
    • The renewal reminders and receipts come straight to you, so it never lapses by accident.
    • It is a business cost. Ask your accountant about claiming it.
    • For a .com.au, Australian rules say the business itself must hold it.
  2. We host your website, free. There is no hosting fee. You get a full copy of your site files when it goes live, and if you ever want your site hosted somewhere else, we move it for you.
  3. Your website is $750, once. No monthly fee for the website. Your web address renewal (about $10 to $25 a year) goes to the company you bought it from.
  4. Changes after your site is live: $49 per change session. We have a quick chat, then email you a recap of what we will change. Once you approve the recap, we make the change and send you an invoice.
  5. Going live. You approve your site in writing. After you have paid, we point your web address at it. Until then, you only see a private draft link.
  6. Your details. What you tell us here is used to build your website. We do not share it.
